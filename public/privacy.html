Privacy notice

  • Reading Wire Room needs no account. Visit counts use GoatCounter, which doesn't use cookies.
  • If you sign in to the Community features (with Google), we keep: your Google sign-in details (name, email address and an account ID), stored by our database host, Supabase; the username, display name and bio you choose; and what you post, comment, follow and report.
  • What's public: your username, display name, bio, posts, comments and who you follow. Your email address is never shown.
  • What we don't do: sell your data, show ads, or track you across other sites. We only use your Google sign-in to identify your account.
  • Deleting your account: any time, from your profile (Edit profile → Delete my account). Your profile, posts, comments and follows are deleted. Reports you sent stay, without your name.
  • Questions or requests: use the Feedback button on the site.

Terms of use

  • You must be 13 or older to have an account.
  • You keep ownership of what you post. By posting, you let Wire Room store and display it on the site and in shared links.
  • You're responsible for what you post. Users' posts are their own views and analysis, labelled Analyst, and aren't checked or endorsed by Wire Room.
  • Wire Room is offered as it is, free, with no guarantee that it will always be available or accurate. Don't rely on it for decisions about anyone's safety.
  • These terms may change; the version date above shows the latest.

Community rules

  • Show your sources. Link the reports your analysis is based on, and say when something is your guess.
  • Argue with ideas, not people. No harassment, hate, slurs or threats, and no cheering on violence.
  • No private information. Don't post anyone's home address, phone number, private photos or other personal details, and don't help find someone.
  • No illegal content, and nothing that helps someone hurt people.
  • Be yourself. Don't pretend to be someone else, a government or a news outlet.
  • No spam, ads or repeated posting of the same thing.
  • Respect copyright. Link to articles and quote a sentence or two at most.

Anyone signed in can report a post, comment or account. Moderators can hide posts and comments and suspend accounts that break these rules, without notice.
